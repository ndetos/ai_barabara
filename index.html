Results to date

Not a concept - a working prototype

Trained, validated, and running today. KURA has already committed KES 14.87M to fund Phase 1.

Model accuracy (mAP50)
0.49
On a balanced 4,165-image subset of RDD2022.
Comparable to published research baselines - achieved with a nano model trained entirely on CPU.
Damage classes detected
4
Longitudinal cracks (D00), Transverse cracks (D10), Alligator cracking (D20), Potholes (D40).
Covers the four dominant failure modes on Kenyan paved urban roads.

Five integrated modules

What the platform does

MODULE 01

Data & Knowledge Base

The foundation of the platform: why open-source data is insufficient for Kenya, the domain-shift problem, and our local pilot imagery collection.

Explore the data →
MODULE 02

ARICS - Road Inspection

Computer-vision inspection of road imagery from smartphone, vehicle-mounted, or drone cameras. Detects four damage classes with confidence scores and GIS chainage. Replaces slow, subjective manual inspection with a repeatable, auditable process.

MODULE 03

Feasibility AI

AI-assisted cost estimation and economic evaluation using an HDM-4 reduced-form engine. Produces NPV, Benefit-Cost Ratio, IRR, and scenario comparison over a user-defined analysis period — the same outputs a consumer expects from a full feasibility study.

MODULE 04

Existing Software Study

Institutional AI-readiness assessment covering data availability, governance, ICT infrastructure, and capacity — establishing the baseline for integration with KURA's existing systems.

Try the assessment →
MODULE 05

Dashboard

Live KPIs, defect distribution, section condition, alerts, and maintenance priority ranking for engineers and management. Decision-support, not autonomous decisions — every output is reviewable and traceable.

VALIDATION

Model Validation Report

A transparent, honest look at the ARICS model's performance. Metrics, confusion matrix, failure modes, and the compute constraints that define the next phase of work.

View validation report →

Built to a nine-stage, human-in-the-loop methodology aligned with Kenya Roads Design Manuals, FIDIC, HDM-4, and the Data Protection Act, 2019. The full methodology document (v1.0, September 2026) is available on request.

What we are seeking

Enabling conditions for scale

KURA has committed KES 14.87 million for Phase 1 - a 24-month programme covering development, pilot, and knowledge transfer. That commitment funds the proof of concept. The transition to national and continental deployment requires three enabling conditions we are seeking through the ECA Innovation Café.

What Phase 1 delivers vs. what remains

Phase 1 - KURA-funded (KES 14.87M)

  • Functional prototypes of ARICS, Feasibility AI, and Dashboard
  • 40–50 km of Kenyan road imagery collected with GPS metadata
  • Institutional AI-readiness assessment
  • Three-month pilot with a KURA case project
  • Testing, validation, training, and knowledge transfer

What remains - the ask

  • Compute access. Sustained GPU resources for retraining on Kenyan imagery with larger model variants.
  • Nationwide imagery collection. Beyond the pilot corridor, national deployment requires orders of magnitude more data.
  • Policy engagement and partnerships. Guidance on how road agencies procure and deploy AI - plus linkages to other African road agencies, development finance institutions, and research institutions.
