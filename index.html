Four integrated modules

One platform, the full road management cycle

MODULE 01

ARICS — Road Inspection

Computer-vision inspection of road imagery — vehicle-mounted, drone, or smartphone — detecting four road damage classes with confidence scores and GIS chainage. Replaces slow, subjective manual inspection.

MODULE 02

Feasibility AI

AI-assisted cost estimation and economic evaluation using an HDM-4 reduced-form engine. Produces NPV, Benefit-Cost Ratio, IRR, and scenario comparison over a user-defined analysis period.

MODULE 03

Existing Software Study

Institutional AI-readiness assessment covering data availability, governance, ICT infrastructure, and capacity — establishing the baseline for integration with KURA's existing systems.

MODULE 04

Dashboard

AI-powered reporting for engineers and management: KPIs, alerts, maintenance priority ranking, and management reports. Decision support, not autonomous decisions.

How we build it

A nine-stage, human-in-the-loop methodology

Every AI output is decision-support. Qualified engineers review and approve before any action. Standards-aligned to Kenya Roads Design Manuals, FIDIC, HDM-4, and the Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Inception & Requirements

Project baseline, use cases, requirements specification

2. Existing Software Study

KURA systems inventory, AI readiness assessment

3. Data & Knowledge Base

Data architecture, engineering knowledge base, Kenyan imagery

4a. ARICS AI Module

Computer-vision road inspection with GIS integration

4b. Feasibility AI Module

AI-assisted cost estimation and economic evaluation

5. Integration & Dashboard

System integration and AI-powered reporting

6. Testing & Validation

Unit, integration, model, and engineering validation

7. Pilot Implementation

Controlled pilot with KURA case project

8. Training & Knowledge Transfer

User manuals, technical documentation, train-the-trainer

9. Monitoring & Refinement

Performance monitoring and continuous improvement

What we are seeking

Enabling conditions for scale

KURA has committed KES 14.87 million for Phase 1 — a 24-month development and pilot programme covering staff, workstation procurement, contracted imagery collection, and pilot activities. That commitment funds the proof of concept.

The transition from a successful pilot to a national and continental deployment requires enabling conditions that Phase 1 does not cover. These are the supports we are seeking through the ECA Innovation Café.

What Phase 1 delivers vs. what remains

Phase 1 (KURA-funded, KES 14.87M) delivers:

  • Inception, requirements, and project baseline
  • Existing software study and institutional AI readiness assessment
  • Data architecture and engineering knowledge base
  • 100–150 km of Kenyan road imagery collection with GPS metadata
  • Functional prototypes of ARICS, Feasibility AI, and Dashboard
  • Testing, engineering validation, and User Acceptance Testing
  • Three-month pilot with a KURA case project
  • Training, documentation, and knowledge transfer

What remains beyond Phase 1 — the ask:

  • Compute access. Sustained GPU resources for retraining on Kenyan imagery with larger model variants. Our current model was trained on CPU because free-tier cloud GPU runs were terminated at epochs 5 and 11.
  • Nationwide imagery collection. Beyond the pilot corridor, national deployment requires orders of magnitude more data.
  • Policy engagement. Clear guidance on how road agencies can procure, validate, and deploy AI systems — including data-sharing frameworks and reference standards.
  • Partnership linkages. Connections to other African road agencies, development finance institutions, and research institutions.
